Our Churches

We meet across three buildings in Edinburgh, each with its own part in the life of the community.

Meadow Lane

2 Meadow Lane, Edinburgh, EH8 9NR

The original home of the community. A small chapel where the clergy live, with a hall attached, used for the daily and smaller services during the week.

Open in Google Maps

Chapel Street

33 Chapel Street, Edinburgh, EH8 9AY

The larger church, which hosts the busier services - the Sundays and the great feasts. Being furnished gradually.

Open in Google Maps

Craigmillar Park

2c Craigmillar Park, Edinburgh

The newly purchased property being furnished to become our future main church. It also hosts the Orthodox school and the larger meals and social gatherings.
