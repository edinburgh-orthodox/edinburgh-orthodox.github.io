Catechetical classes are open to everyone and are offered throughout the year by members of the clergy. Find their contact details on the [Clergy page]({{< relref "/clergy" >}}).

### By appointment

In person or via Zoom, with Fr. Antonios Kakalis.

### Sundays

After Coffee Hour, following the Divine Liturgy, led by Fr. Luke Jeffery.

### Teenagers

Catechetical class for teenagers, led by Reader Stephen Griffith. For more information, please contact [stephen4.griffith@gmail.com](mailto:stephen4.griffith@gmail.com).

### Weekdays

Bishop Raphael also conducts catechetical classes during weekdays, please check the annoucements on the homepage for information on the next session.
