The schedule of services for Lent, Holy Week, Pascha and Pentecost 2026, as planned to take place, is below.

All Divine Liturgies marked for 10.00 am are preceded by Matins at 8.30 am. The Liturgies of the Presanctified Gifts on Fridays are preceded by Matins at 6.00 am. No weekly programme is shared during this period other than activities and alterations, so please keep a copy of this schedule.

## February

### The beginning of Holy and Great Lent

- **Mon 23**: Vespers 5.30 pm, and Great Compline with the Great Canon 6.30 pm (Meadow Lane). These two services are held at the same time from Monday to Thursday in the first week of Lent.
- **Wed 25**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Fri 27**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane), and Compline with the Akathist Hymn, Part I [Α΄ Χαιρετισμοί] 6.30 pm (Chapel Street)
- **Sat 28**: St Theodore the Recruit. Liturgy in Greek 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)

## March

- **Sun 1**: Sunday of Orthodoxy, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Wed 4**: Presanctified Liturgy 6.30 pm (Meadow Lane), fast from midnight if possible
- **Fri 6**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane), and Compline with the Akathist Hymn, Part II [Β΄ Χαιρετισμοί] 6.30 pm (Chapel Street)
- **Sun 8**: St Gregory Palamas, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Mon 9**: The Holy Forty Martyrs of Sevasteia, Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Wed 11**: Presanctified Liturgy 6.30 pm (Meadow Lane), fast from midnight if possible
- **Fri 13**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane), and Compline with the Akathist Hymn, Part III [Γ΄ Χαιρετισμοί] 6.30 pm (Chapel Street)
- **Sun 15**: Veneration of the Precious Cross, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Wed 18**: Presanctified Liturgy 6.30 pm (Meadow Lane), fast from midnight if possible
- **Fri 20**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane), and Compline with the Akathist Hymn, Part IV [Δ΄ Χαιρετισμοί] 6.30 pm (Chapel Street)
- **Sat 21**: Liturgy in Slavonic 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sun 22**: St John Climacus, and Greek National Day [Εθνική Εορτή]. Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Wed 25**: The Annunciation of the Theotokos, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Thu 26**: Compline with the Great Canon in full 6.30 pm (Meadow Lane)
- **Fri 27**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane), and the Akathist Hymn 6.30 pm (Chapel Street)
- **Sat 28**: Saturday of the Akathist Hymn, and the annual memorial service for Fr John Maitland Moir. Liturgy in Greek 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sun 29**: St Mary of Egypt, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)

## April

- **Wed 1**: Presanctified Liturgy 6.30 pm (Meadow Lane), fast from midnight if possible
- **Fri 3**: Presanctified Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sat 4**: Lazarus Saturday, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Sun 5**: Palm Sunday [Βαΐων], Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)

### Holy and Great Week

All services are at Chapel Street.

- **Sun 5**: Vespers 5.30 pm, and the Bridegroom Service [Ακολουθία τοῦ Νυμφίου] 6.30 pm
- **Mon 6**: Vespers 5.30 pm, and the Bridegroom Service 6.30 pm
- **Tue 7**: Vespers 5.30 pm, and the Bridegroom Service 6.30 pm
- **Wed 8**: Vespers 5.30 pm, and the Service of the Anointing 6.30 pm
- **Thu 9**: Matins 8.30 am, Vespers and the Liturgy of St Basil 10.00 am, and the Service of the Passion of Our Lord, the Twelve Gospels, 6.30 pm
- **Fri 10**: The Descent from the Cross, Great Hours and Vespers, 9.00 am to 12.00 noon, and the Service of the Lord's Burial [Ἐπιτάφιος] 6.30 pm
- **Sat 11**: The Lord's Descent to Hades, Vespers and the Liturgy of St Basil [Πρώτη Ἀνάστασις] 9.00 am, and the Paschal Vigil 11.00 pm

### Pascha and Bright Week

- **Sun 12**: Matins and the Divine Liturgy of Holy Pascha, following the Paschal Vigil, and the Vespers of Love 12.00 noon (Chapel Street)
- **Mon 13**: Second day of Pascha, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Tue 14**: SS Raphael, Nicholas and Irene, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Fri 17**: The Life-Bearing Spring [Ζωοδόχου Πηγῆς], Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sat 18**: St Cuthbert, Divine Liturgy in Slavonic 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)

### After Bright Week

- **Sun 19**: St Thomas, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Tue 21**: Radonitsa, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Thu 23**: St George, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sat 25**: St Mark the Apostle, Liturgy in Greek 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sun 26**: The Myrrh-bearing Women, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)

## May

- **Sun 3**: The Paralytic, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Wed 6**: Mid-Pentecost, Divine Liturgy 7.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Thu 7**: Vigil for St John the Theologian 9.00 pm (Meadow Lane)
- **Sun 10**: The Samaritan Woman, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street), and the Annual General Meeting 1.15 pm
- **Sun 17**: The Blind Man, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Wed 20**: Vigil for the Leave-taking of Pascha 9.00 pm (Meadow Lane)
- **Thu 21**: The Ascension of the Lord, and SS Constantine and Helen, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sat 23**: St Evmenios, Liturgy in Greek 10.00 am (Meadow Lane)
- **Sat 30**: Saturday of Souls, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)
- **Sun 31**: Pentecost, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)

## June

- **Mon 1**: Monday of the Holy Spirit, Divine Liturgy 10.00 am (Chapel Street)

## Notes

- Meadow Lane is 2 Meadow Lane, Edinburgh EH8 9NR. Chapel Street is 33 Chapel Street, Edinburgh EH8 9AY.
- Divine Liturgies at 10.00 am are preceded by Matins at 8.30 am.
- Divine Liturgies and Presanctified Liturgies at 7.00 am are preceded by Matins at 6.00 am.
- On the Wednesdays of Great Lent marked above, the fast is, if possible, from midnight, a minimum of nine hours.
- There are no memorial services from 4 to 19 April.
