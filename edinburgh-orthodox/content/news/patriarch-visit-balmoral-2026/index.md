His All-Holiness Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew met His Majesty King Charles III in the morning of Monday 21 September 2026, at Balmoral Castle in Scotland.

This was their second meeting since the British monarch's accession to the throne. Their first meeting took place in October 2022, and in the past the Patriarch and the then Prince of Wales had also met in London.

By way of exception, the King received the Patriarch in a private meeting at his personal summer residence, Balmoral Castle. Their conversation was originally scheduled to last half an hour, and was extended by a further half hour in a very cordial and friendly atmosphere.

![King Charles III and Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew standing together at Balmoral](king1.jpg)

Upon the departure of His All-Holiness the Patriarch, the King personally accompanied him to the exit of the Castle.

His All-Holiness was accompanied to this meeting by His Grace Bishop Raphael of Ilion, who ministers in Scotland, and the Very Reverend Grand Ecclesiarch Aetios, director of the Patriarch's Private Office.
