### Walk-in confessions

Bishop Raphael holds walk-in confessions on Wednesdays from 1:00pm to 4:00pm at Meadow Lane. Please check the announcements for the week for possible changes to these hours or a cancellation.

### By appointment

Fr. Antonios Kakalis, Fr. Luke Jeffery and Fr. John Nankivell also hear confessions. Please speak to them directly to make an appointment when you are next in church, or find their contact details on the [Clergy page]({{< relref "/clergy" >}}).
