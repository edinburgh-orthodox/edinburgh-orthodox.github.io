{{/* Renders the page collection passed as context as news cards with a front image. The image comes from the `image` front matter key, resolved as a page bundle resource or a site path. Posts without one get `cover*` from their bundle, or the St Andrew icon, so every card keeps the same shape. Bundle images are cropped to the 16:10 card shape at up to three widths. */}} {{ $fallback := resources.Get "images/st-andrew-icon.jpg" }}
